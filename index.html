African Choir

Goal : Musical Evangelism

The African Choir is open to anyone interested in singing and participating in the church's musical activities.

This ministry started at St. Cecilia in 2018 with a small group of 6 parishioners and later moved to New All Saints Church in 2025. Meetings are held on Fridays at 7:00 pm and Saturdays at 4:00 pm.
